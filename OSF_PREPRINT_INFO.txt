[Step 1] Title and Abstract
- Title:
RIMAN RESEARCH: Clinical & Microclimate Evaluation of INCELLDERM Dermatology Active Cream EX in the Philippine Tropical Zone

- Abstract (内嵌 GitHub 仓库入口、交互式报告及双基准 DOI):
Background: The Philippine tropical microclimate (77-81% RH) poses significant challenges to traditional emulsion stability and skin barrier function. Methods: A stratified survey of N=200 consumers and B2B practitioners assessed the performance of INCELLDERM's Dermatology Active Cream EX, focusing on its Giant BYoungPool (57% Centella Asiatica Leaf Water) patent and NBP Complex. Results: The formulation achieved a composite mean of 3.99 with an 82.2% Top-2-Box score. Notably, texture lightweightness scored 4.24 (98.5% T2B), while Ceramide NP barrier reinforcement confidence reached 4.47 (98.0% T2B). However, comfort under makeup during peak heat showed a lower mean of 3.22. Strategic Implications: While peptide anti-aging claims drive purchase intent (73.5%), 'texture too heavy' remains a perceived barrier (99.5% selection rate), indicating a need for educational marketing on absorption kinetics. The study validates the product's suitability for post-procedure care (mean 4.07) and highlights strong B2B adoption among aesthetic clinic owners (mean 4.17).
