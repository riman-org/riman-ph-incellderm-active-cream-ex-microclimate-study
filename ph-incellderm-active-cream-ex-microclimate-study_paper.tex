\begin{abstract}
Background: The Philippine tropical microclimate (77-81\% RH) poses significant challenges to traditional emulsion stability and skin barrier function. Methods: A stratified survey of N=200 consumers and B2B practitioners assessed the performance of INCELLDERM's Dermatology Active Cream EX, focusing on its Giant BYoungPool (57\% Centella Asiatica Leaf Water) patent and NBP Complex. Results: The formulation achieved a composite mean of 3.99 with an 82.2\% Top-2-Box score. Notably, texture lightweightness scored 4.24 (98.5\% T2B), while Ceramide NP barrier reinforcement confidence reached 4.47 (98.0\% T2B). However, comfort under makeup during peak heat showed a lower mean of 3.22. Strategic Implications: While peptide anti-aging claims drive purchase intent (73.5\%), 'texture too heavy' remains a perceived barrier (99.5\% selection rate), indicating a need for educational marketing on absorption kinetics. The study validates the product's suitability for post-procedure care (mean 4.07) and highlights strong B2B adoption among aesthetic clinic owners (mean 4.17).

\vspace{0.4em}
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} RIMAN Skincare, INCELLDERM, Dermatology Active Cream EX, Microclimate Adaptation, NBP Complex, Philippine Market
\end{abstract}

\section{1. Formulation Architecture \& Study Methodology}
This study evaluates the INCELLDERM Dermatology Active Cream EX, formerly known as Active Cream EX, within the specific context of the Philippine tropical environment. The core formulation relies on the patented Giant BYoungPool technology, utilizing 57\% Centella Asiatica Leaf Water, combined with the NBP Complex and key peptides including Acetyl Hexapeptide-8 and Palmitoyl Pentapeptide-4. The methodology involved a stratified sample of 200 participants across Metro Manila, Cebu City, and Davao, representing varying relative humidity levels from 77\% to 81\%. Participants included both C-end consumers and B2B stakeholders such as aesthetic clinic owners and cross-border e-commerce sellers. The assessment framework utilized Likert-scale questions for subjective sensory metrics and single-choice/multi-select questions for behavioral patterns and purchase drivers. This rigorous approach allows for a granular analysis of how advanced Korean dermatological formulations perform against local environmental stressors.
\section{2. Quantitative Performance \& Microclimate Adaptation}
The empirical data reveals a robust overall composite mean of 3.99, with an impressive 82.2\% Top-2-Box satisfaction rate. Specific metrics highlight the product's strength in texture perception; the statement regarding lightweight, non-sticky feel in high humidity received a mean score of 4.24, with 98.5\% of respondents agreeing or strongly agreeing. Similarly, confidence in Ceramide NP’s ability to reinforce the skin barrier against environmental stressors was exceptionally high at 4.47 (98.0\% T2B). Absorption speed also performed well with a mean of 4.02. However, a critical area for improvement is identified in wearing comfort under makeup during hot days, which scored a lower mean of 3.22. Hydration duration data indicates that 63\% of users experience effects lasting 8-12 hours, while 22.5\% report benefits exceeding 12 hours, validating the '12-Hour Barrier Retention' claim for the majority of the user base.
\section{3. Cross-Demographic, MBTI \& B2B/C-End Stratification}
Segmentation analysis provides deep insights into user behavior. By skin type, Post-Procedure Fragile users reported the highest satisfaction (mean 4.07), followed closely by Normal/Dehydrated Urban types (4.04), suggesting strong clinical applicability. In terms of occupation, B2B Aesthetic Clinic Owners demonstrated the highest endorsement (mean 4.17), significantly outperforming other segments, indicating strong professional trust in the brand's efficacy. Demographically, Affluent/High Net Worth individuals showed higher satisfaction (4.22) compared to Lower-Middle Income groups (3.80), correlating with price sensitivity. Psychographic profiling via MBTI revealed that ENFJ personalities (mean 4.16) and ENTJs (4.12) were most receptive, likely due to their leadership-oriented and results-driven nature. Purchase drivers are heavily influenced by the Centella soothing patent (75\%) and peptide anti-aging claims (73.5\%). Conversely, the primary barrier to adoption is the perception of 'texture too heavy in humidity' (99.5\% selection rate), despite actual test scores showing lightness, highlighting a gap between pre-conception and post-use reality.
\section{4. Regulatory Compliance \& Regional Expansion Strategy}
Strategic expansion in the Philippines requires addressing regulatory and logistical nuances. The study confirms high trust in safety and compliance (mean 3.94, 86\% T2B), which is crucial for navigating the FDA Philippines requirements for imported cosmetics. Competitor benchmarking shows that 57\% of users compare this product against K-Beauty moisturizers, positioning it firmly within the premium Korean skincare category rather than Western dermatological creams (22.5\%). To overcome the 'availability in local stores' barrier (23.5\%), RIMAN Mall should leverage digital-first distribution channels favored by the high-engagement B2B segment. Marketing communications must pivot to educate consumers on the rapid absorption kinetics to counteract the 'heavy texture' misconception. Furthermore, emphasizing the clinical backing of the NBP Complex and the ethical sourcing of Centella Asiatica can appeal to the sensitive INFP and ISFP demographics who value brand story and sensory aesthetics.

\section{Conclusion}
The RIMAN RESEARCH study validates INCELLDERM Dermatology Active Cream EX as a highly effective solution for the Philippine tropical microclimate, particularly for post-procedure care and barrier repair. With a composite mean of 3.99 and exceptional scores for texture lightness (4.24) and Ceramide NP efficacy (4.47), the product demonstrates strong scientific merit. However, the discrepancy between the high actual performance and the perceived barrier of 'heavy texture' (99.5\% concern) necessitates targeted consumer education. The strong endorsement from B2B aesthetic professionals (4.17) serves as a powerful credibility anchor. Future strategies should focus on digital accessibility and transparent communication of absorption rates to convert passive users into promoters, thereby improving the current NPS of -1.5.
